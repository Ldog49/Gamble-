# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A small group of friends (roughly 4-6 people) who each place a £5 bet on one
Premier League match every gameweek via bet365. They use the app casually on
their phones — usually a quick check after uploading a slip, or a glance at
the group's running scoreboard during the week. No professional or financial
context; this is a fun side thing between mates, not a serious betting or
finance tool.

## Product Purpose

Turns a photo of a bet365 slip into a tracked, graded bet, and rolls the
group's weekly bets up into a shared scoreboard: who's up, who's down, and
the season-long story of who's winning the group. Success looks like: someone
opens the app after their team's match and immediately knows if they won,
and can see at a glance how they stack up against their mates this season.

## Brand Personality

Fun, matchday energy. Playful and a little competitive — like a group chat
scoreboard, not a finance dashboard. Confident and clear about wins/losses
(no hedging), with some personality in copy and states, but never at the
expense of the numbers being instantly readable. Green reads as "pitch" and
"profit" at once.

## Anti-references

- Not a corporate SaaS dashboard (no hero-metric gradient cards, no generic
  "enterprise analytics" look).
- Not a serious/clinical betting-shop or trading-terminal aesthetic — this
  isn't a real-money professional tool.
- Not generic AI-template look: no default shadcn-blue, no evenly-spaced
  identical icon+heading cards, no gradient text.

## Design Principles

- Win/loss reads instantly — color plus label plus icon, never color alone.
- Mobile-first: bets are uploaded and checked from a phone, most sessions
  are quick glances, not long dashboard sessions.
- Green is earned, not decorative — it marks positive profit, live/active
  states, and primary actions, not used as background wallpaper.
- Numbers stay trustworthy under the fun tone — playful chrome, precise data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA contrast for all text and interactive elements. Win/loss/
push states are distinguished by label and icon as well as color, so they
remain legible for colorblind users. Respects light/dark system theme.
